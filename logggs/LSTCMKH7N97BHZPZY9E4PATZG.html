    \nTypes of Coffee Maker Machines \xe2\x80\x93 Filter Machines and Percolators

    \n\xc2\xa0\n\n

    Filter machines brew drip coffee into a jar or carafe using water heated at the top that drips through ground coffee. These come with permanent or paper filters. Paper filters are easy to use but need replacing. Permanent filters are cheaper long term but need more cleaning.

    \n\n

    Percolators work by boiling water that bubbles up through a tube and drips through coffee powder. These come in stovetop or electric versions.

    \n\n


    \nCoffee Maker Machine \xe2\x80\x93 Cappuccino and Espresso Makers

    \n\n

    These steam driven machines make strong coffee. You can get pressure driven or pump driven models.
