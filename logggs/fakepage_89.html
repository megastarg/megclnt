Using a fan as the heating mechanism, this appliance cooks food with hot air inside a tray. The quick air movement ensures that the food is cooked from the inside out. The high heat speeds up the cooking process and makes the food crispier. Despite its name, an air fryer can not only air fry, but also cook steaks, grill fish and meat, bake cakes and cookies, and roast your favourite meals, making it ideal for cooking enthusiasts or those looking to cut down on cooking time.
